# Claims and limitations — October 4 clarification cycle

The software provides reproducible numerical recommendations under declared equivalent circuits. It does not establish the physical CPRI generator's waveform accuracy, approved wiring or safe operating settings. No genuine CPRI measured trace was found in the inspected sources.

## Explicit answers to the ten required technical questions

1. **Did 520 Ω change LI feasibility?** Availability alone did not. At the same illustrative load, expanding the single-resistor catalog from 56 to 504 gives zero LI passes. The explicit 180 || 520 hypothesis adds three conditional passes among 84 additional configurations. The inventory clarification makes that component-based hypothesis possible, but does not confirm it.
2. **Can the briefing LI case be produced?** The illustrative 1550 kV request can be produced numerically within the stated parallel-tail equivalent. Nine stages at 184.238331 kV/stage, 30 Ω fronts and 180 || 520 Ω tails give T1=1.296891 µs and T2=50.908673 µs. The fixed modeled load is 850 pF DUT + 500 pF divider + 150 pF stray + 480 pF additional basic load, with L=18.5 µH. The briefing's physical 400 kV equipment setup is insufficiently specified to claim it has been reproduced.
3. **Which arrangements are confirmed?** No complete connection recipe is directly confirmed. The six component values are confirmed. The uniform reduction and parallel equivalent are mathematical derivations conditional on their graphs. Both shunt placements and the parallel-tail connection remain hypotheses; the latter is off by default.
4. **What changed in physics?** Versioned inventory/profile, bounded recipes and BOM, explicit second tail branch, independent full-stage validation, a guarded modal solution of the same passive ODE, and a root-bracketing roundoff fix. Shared waveform definitions, limits, declared load and charge/energy bounds were not relaxed. A nominal target generator uses the same evaluator.
5. **Which artifacts were invalidated?** Old simulation/ML artifacts retain validity for their original assumptions but are incompatible with current serving provenance and incomplete for the expanded domain. The old simulation v1 dataset/registry/results, old physics/ML examples, old 56-candidate recommendations and obsolete documents are preserved outside active runtime. Historical workbook reproduction remains labelled and isolated. New serving uses clarification_v2 / simulation_v2 only.
6. **Did positive coverage improve?** LI shape-positive synthetic cases increased from one in the former design to 167 (one single-tail, 166 parallel-tail). LI/OSHUNT still has no positive timing cases. These are deliberately stratified design counts, not success probabilities. Of all 18,339 attempts, 17 satisfy full randomly assigned crest-and-time conditions; optimizer charge solving has a different role.
7. **What is ML's final value?** Four trained residual Extra Trees routes support real batch exploration and separate scalar predictions. The measured warm 128-scenario batch is 0.446 s versus 1.455 s for current modal physics, about 3.26×. Cold loading was about 1.4 s and initially removed nearly all advantage. Analytic L=0 estimates are much faster and have better SI tail error; ML's strongest evidence is finite-L LI emulation and bulk exploration, not universal superiority. The final optimizer still verifies every candidate using physics.
8. **Is search exhaustive?** Yes, over the declared catalog, fixed topology/load and analytic legal charge treatment: 504 default candidates, 588 for LI/GSHUNT with both selected hypotheses, or 84 for the pair hypothesis alone. It is not exhaustive over every mathematically possible resistor network or every real CPRI assembly. No approved recipe exists, so approved-only scope has zero candidates.
9. **Are recommendations made from actual values?** All named component parts are among the six confirmed values. Availability checks require both tail parts for the hypothesis and BOM counts overlaps. Required quantities are computed, but available quantities, holders and ratings are unknown; therefore numerical feasibility is not practical installation approval.
10. **What needs measured data?** Absolute crest/timing accuracy on the real generator, residual calibration, uncertainty coverage, robustness to load/polarity and switching variation, shot repeatability, instrument-chain effects and operational recommendations. Neither synthetic holdout accuracy nor full-stage equivalence answers these questions.

## What is deliberately not claimed

- No laboratory validation, hardware approval, safe pulse ratings, reliable low-voltage firing or standards certification. The 50 kV SI example's approximately 8.103 kV/stage setting illustrates the unknown firing minimum rather than solving it.
- No guarantee that the true best CPRI configuration is inside the bounded uniform catalog. Nonuniform allocations, unconfirmed mixed-stage designs and arbitrary resistor-network synthesis were excluded.
- No fitted empirical calibration or automatic measured-data training. Uploaded provenance can be claimed or erroneous. Clean-curve diagnostics are explicitly unqualified, and genuine CPRI source identity requires independent authentication.
- No qualified treatment of overshoot/noise/chopped impulses, de-embedding or certified instrument uncertainty. Unsupported records remain unsupported. No thresholds were softened to force a pass.
- No calibrated statistical confidence from empirical validation P95 errors. A 576-case screen had no full positives and cannot establish false-rejection performance; 61 of 64 unsupported waveforms received scalar estimates, showing why scalar screening cannot establish waveform quality.
- No claim that ML always improves decisions: SI analytic tail errors are slightly lower, and an actual synthetic gain discrepancy makes ML falsely accept a deliberately marginal crest. The UI shows the reference failure and keeps it authoritative.
- No claim that a simulation-specific numerical software test is independent laboratory evidence. The independent optimizer oracle has its own enumeration, charge and ranking arithmetic but shares the physics backend; Radau and full-stage checks provide additional numerical cross-checks.

## Measurement work needed next

CPRI should first identify the fired circuit, actual per-stage resistor placements/quantities and pulse ratings, basic-load coverage, auxiliary states, minimum stable charge and charge increment. Then acquire raw signed time/voltage exports with unambiguous units, actual settings, DUT/divider/stray information, measurement plane, calibrated divider/digitizer/channel identities, acquisition clocks and repeat shot series.

Reserve complete setup families and shot series for independent assessment; never split neighboring repeats across training and test. Independently qualify curves and uncertainty before residual calibration. Freeze a new physical profile/model version only after comparing predictions with these traces. This release's development gate of ten independent setups is an explicit software minimum, not proof of sufficient laboratory coverage or a metrology standard.
